PROBLEM STATEMENT Team Apex | SkillBridge AI

The Academia–Industry Disconnect in Campus Hiring

Higher education institutions in Uttarakhand and across India face massive structural friction connecting student skills with industry demand.

1

No Shared Skill-Mapping Standard

Academia measures capability via marks and course credits; industry measures it via role-fit and practical code readiness. With no standardized skill map, both sides operate blindly.

2

Internships Sourced by Cold Outreach

Placement cells (TPOs) and students chase internships manually company-by-company, while recruiters struggle to discover verified student talent from regional colleges outside metro cities.

3

Placement is Tracked, Not Managed

Institutions log placement outcomes after the fact in static spreadsheets, with zero live analytics connecting student learning activity to recruiter decisions.

4

No Unified Tri-Party Portal

Students use job boards, recruiters use bloated ATS tools, and colleges use spreadsheets — creating fragmented silos that cause talent wastage and long hiring cycles.

INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS Team Apex | SkillBridge AI

Why SkillBridge Beats Generic Job Boards & LMSs

Traditional job portals show raw resumes; traditional LMSs stop at course video completion. SkillBridge connects both into a closed-loop hiring engine.

01

One Skill Graph, 3 Lenses

The same verified skill dataset powers student readiness, recruiter search rankings, and institute curriculum insights — no data duplicate entry.

02

AI Reads the Gap, Not Just Resume

Compares candidate skills directly against job requirements to output a personalized, time-boxed action plan to achieve 100% readiness.

03

Built-in 2-Round Assessment Engine

Timed MCQ screening with negative marking gating an automated live coding challenge — recruiters receive pre-evaluated candidates.

04

Closed-Loop Feedback System

Institutions get real-time feedback on which technical skills cause candidate rejection, turning hiring data into curriculum upgrades.

05

Learning Tied to Verified Outcomes

Completing targeted micro-courses instantly updates candidate readiness scores and notifies recruiters watching that skill matrix.

06

Recruiter-Grade Match Score

Students see exact transparent AI match percentages for roles (e.g. 94% fit), identical to the score ranking shown to recruiters.
